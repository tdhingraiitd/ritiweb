A delightful, friction-free shopping experience for a growing D2C brand — lifting conversion by 28%.

Role
Product & Visual Designer
Duration
3 months
Year
2023
Category
Mobile · Commerce
Problem

A fast-growing direct-to-consumer brand had a clunky checkout and low repeat-purchase rates. Browsing felt generic and the path to purchase leaked customers at every step.

They needed an experience as warm and considered as their products.

Process

I reimagined product discovery and browsing, then rebuilt checkout into a fast, reassuring flow with clear progress and trust signals. A warm, tactile visual identity carried through every screen, giving the brand a distinct, premium feel.

Quick rounds of testing validated the new flows before launch.
